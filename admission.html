B.Tech. (Computer Science & Design)

Program Overview

The B.Tech. / BCA / B.sc (Computer Science and Design) program at Vidyapeeth equips students with the technical expertise and design thinking skills to create innovative solutions in the ever-evolving field of computer science.

The curriculum provides a strong foundation in computer science fundamentals, programming languages, algorithms, data structures, and software engineering principles. Students also gain in-depth knowledge of user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) design, enabling them to design user-centric and visually appealing applications.

This program prepares graduates for a wide range of careers in the IT industry, including software development, web development, user interface design, user experience design, and more.

Programme Fee

The tuition fee for the B.Tech. (Computer Science and Design) program is ₹1,65,000 per year. This excludes eligibility fee, examination fee, convocation fee, and hostel charges (if applicable).

We understand that affordability is a major concern. Vidyapeeth offers a variety of scholarships and financial aid options to help deserving students pursue their academic goals. Visit the "Financial Aid" section of our website for more information.
