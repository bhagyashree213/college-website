Program Overview: B.Tech. in Computer Science & Design

The B.Tech. (Computer Science and Design) program at Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth equips students with advanced technical expertise and human-centered design thinking skills to create innovative solutions in the ever-evolving field of computer science and digital experiences.

The curriculum provides a solid foundation in software engineering fundamentals, algorithms, data structures, cloud architectures, artificial intelligence, and user experience (UI/UX) design. Students gain hands-on proficiency in building interactive web, mobile, and software systems.

Graduates are prepared for top roles in the technology and design industries, including Software Development Engineer, Full Stack Developer, Product Designer, UI/UX Architect, and System Analyst.
