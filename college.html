About Our Institution

The Dr. D. Y. Patil School of Science & Technology at Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth (DPU) is a forward-looking technological institution offering undergraduate programs in cutting-edge branches including B.Tech. in Computer Science & Design and B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

DPU envisions being one of the largest and most respected learning universities in India, delivering quality education to aspiring students with an emphasis on hands-on practical skills and creative problem-solving. To foster a wholesome learning environment, world-class computational labs, smart digital classrooms, and a sprawling green campus have been established at Tathawade, Pune.

To ensure academic and industry relevance, DPU has onboarded an esteemed faculty team alongside industry veterans for webinars, hands-on workshops, and collaborative capstone projects. Research domain heads continuously align the curriculum with emerging global technology trends.
